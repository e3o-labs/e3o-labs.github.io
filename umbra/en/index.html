Umbra

Keep the light of your day.

A quiet notebook for your thoughts, photos and the light around them.

Umbra’s App Store release is approved. If the store page does not appear yet, please try again later. Six languages are supported; Android is in preparation.

01

Your place, your light

Choose a city and time zone, then place a branch, cactus, pebble or your photo object beside the notebook. Sunlight and moonlight follow the place and time you choose; a candle or glowing pebble can accompany the night.

02

A thought and its scene

Save writing, photos and the light of the moment together. Reopen a note to revisit its saved scene. Older entries with incomplete light information are labeled as estimated; later reflections keep their own time.

03

Photos with a gentle atmosphere

Photos can carry a gentle atmosphere based on the saved light and weather. A retained pre-filter copy remains available. Light and weather are artistic interpretations, not measurements of a room or the weather.

A notebook beside the light

On a wide Duo inner display, writing and the light scene sit side by side. Drag or press the handle above the note to swap sides. Folding returns to a single notebook; unfolding restores your chosen side. Your words and saved scenes stay unchanged.
