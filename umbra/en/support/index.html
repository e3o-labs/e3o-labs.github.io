Umbra support

Updated November 18, 2026 · Based on Umbra iOS test build 1.0.1 (25)

iOS 1.0.1 (25) is a TestFlight test build with six languages. TestFlight access is by invitation. Public App Store and Google Play downloads are not yet available.

For questions about entries, photos, backups or using Umbra, contact the support address below.

Operator: 이응랩스(OLabs)

redsunjin@gmail.com

What to include

Tell us the app version, device model, operating system, the screen involved, steps to reproduce and the error message. Redact private writing, photos and location from screenshots. Do not send passwords, verification codes, sensitive entries, precise location or your original backup file.

Missing entries and backups

Check that you are using the same device and app or browser, then check search and trash. Entries, photos and saved light scenes stay locally; there is no automatic account-based sync. Back up before reinstalling, clearing data or changing devices.

JSON backups include saved entries, reflections, photo objects, attachments, pre-filter copies and saved light scenes. Drafts are excluded. The total limit is 10MiB. Restore new backups with the current app and avoid downgrading. Permanently deleted entries cannot be restored in the app; old backup files may still contain them and can add them again. Manage backup files separately.

Duo, rotation and the keyboard

Use the handle above the note to drag or press the notebook to the other side. On the folded outer screen it becomes one notebook. If reporting a layout problem, tell us whether the device was open, partly open or closed, its orientation and whether the keyboard was visible. Keep your draft and export a backup before trying destructive troubleshooting.

Light, photos and weather

Reopened entries show their saved photo or light scene, rather than replacing it with today’s light. Older entries with incomplete light data are marked as estimated. A pre-filter photo is a retained app copy and may not have the original file’s resolution. Photo objects used by entries, trash, editing drafts or the current scene are protected from deletion.

Location permission is optional: choose a city or enter coordinates and a time zone instead. MET Norway forecasts are off by default. Enabling them sends rounded coordinates and your IP address to the provider. Forecasts and artistic effects can differ from actual conditions; snow effects do not measure snow on the ground.

Language

Test build 1.0.1 (25) supports Korean, English, Japanese, Spanish, French and Italian. Choose a language at the bottom of the main screen. Your writing, custom names and saved scenes are not translated or recalculated.
